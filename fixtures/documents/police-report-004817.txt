NARRATIVE
Vehicle A was stationary at the red traffic light on Wibautstraat, heading south, waiting to turn onto Ringdijk. Vehicle B, travelling in the same direction, failed to stop and struck the rear of Vehicle A. The driver of Vehicle B stated that he braked late because the light changed and that Vehicle A "moved backwards" just before the impact. The driver of Vehicle A stated that she was stationary. Witness R. Bakker stated that Vehicle B approached at speed and did not appear to slow down. Damage to the rear bumper and left tail lamp of Vehicle A. Damage to the front bumper and bonnet of Vehicle B. Both vehicles were moved to the side of the road. Vehicle A was driven home by its driver.

No fine issued at the scene. Report filed 14 September 2026, 00:25.
