What happened
On Sunday evening 13 September I was driving home from my sister's place in Watergraafsmeer. I stopped at the red light on the Wibautstraat where you turn onto the Ringdijk. The light was red for a long time. When it turned green I lifted my foot off the brake to move to the accelerator and I think my car rolled back slightly, maybe half a metre, because the road goes up a little there. Right at that moment the car behind me hit me. I felt a hard bang and my bag flew off the passenger seat.

The other driver got out and was angry. He said I had reversed into him. I did not reverse. He gave the police his name and the name of his insurer but he did not have his papers with him.

A cyclist who saw it stopped and gave his name to the police.

My car could still be driven and I drove it home slowly. I took it to Autoherstel Oost on Tuesday morning.

I was not injured. My neck was a bit stiff the next day but that is gone now.
